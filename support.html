❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Didn't find your answer? Ask Ananya directly in the chat box above — she is available 24x7.

How long will my order take to arrive?
Standard delivery takes an average of 60-90 minutes. Slots are available daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM — choose your slot at checkout. Slight delays are possible during rush hours (6-9 PM evenings) and the festive season, and you are kept updated by SMS/WhatsApp.
How much is delivery — are there any hidden charges?
Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 and above. Orders below ₹499 carry a ₹29 handling fee. The final amount is always shown before you pay — there are never any hidden charges. Full details on the Delivery & Shipping page.
How do I track where my order is?
A confirmation SMS/WhatsApp arrives as soon as your order is confirmed. A notification is sent at every step: Picking → Packed → Out for delivery → Delivered. If you still need the status, ask Ananya "where is my order" in the chat box above, or simply give us a call.
What if the vegetables or fruits are not fresh?
Under our freshness guarantee: report it with a photo within 24 hours of delivery — we will send an instant replacement or a 100% refund. No questions asked. This policy applies to every fresh item, dairy and bakery product.
Will I get my money back if I cancel?
Absolutely. You can cancel any time before delivery at no charge — full refund. For prepaid orders the refund reflects in the original payment method within 3-5 working days (usually the same/next day for UPI). COD orders are refunded via UPI/bank transfer.
Which payment options are available?
UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, net banking, wallets and Cash on Delivery — all supported. Every online payment is encrypted (SSL) and processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways; we never store your card details.
What happens if an item is out of stock?
In that case we inform you first — you can accept a substitute, skip the item or take a refund. We never send a different, more expensive item without telling you.
Do you deliver to my area?
Enter your PIN code/area at checkout — you will know instantly. If your area is not on the list yet, let us know on WhatsApp (+91 63931 96765) — we will try to add it as soon as possible.
When is support available?
Ananya AI chat: 24x7 (instant answers).
Phone & WhatsApp support: daily 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
Email (support@rkgrocerymart.com): reply within 24 hours.
🕒 Daily 7 AM – 10 PM 💬 Chat 24x7 📧 Reply in 24 hrs
Can I add or change something in my order?
You can modify or cancel the order before picking begins. Once picking has started a change is not possible (so that your slot is not missed) — you can cancel and place a new order, and the previous one is automatically refunded.
