❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Jawaab nahi mila? Neeche chat box mein Ananya se seedha poochh lein — wo 24x7 available hai.

Mera order kitne time mein pahunchega?
Standard delivery average 60-90 minute mein ho jaati hai. Slots roz 7:00 AM se 10:00 PM tak available hain — checkout par apna slot chunein. Rush hours (shaam 6-9 PM) aur festive season mein thoda delay ho sakta hai, uske liye aapko SMS/WhatsApp se update milta rehta hai.
Delivery kitne ki hai — koi hidden charge to nahi?
₹499 ya usse zyada ke orders par delivery free hai. ₹499 se kam ke orders par ₹29 handling lagta hai. Checkout par final amount pehle hi dikh jaata hai — koi hidden charge kabhi nahi. Poora details: Delivery & Shipping page par.
Kaise track karun ki mera order kahan hai?
Order confirm hote hi confirmation SMS/WhatsApp aata hai. Picking → Packed → Out for delivery → Delivered — har step par notification bheji jaati hai. Agar phir bhi status chahiye to upar chat box mein Ananya se "mera order kahan hai" poochh lein, ya seedha call kar lein.
Sabzi/fruit fresh nahi nikla to kya karun?
Hamari freshness guarantee ke tehat: delivery ke 24 ghante ke andar photo ke saath batayein — hum turant replacement bhejenge ya 100% refund kar denge. Bina kisi sawaal ke. Yeh policy har fresh item, dairy aur bakery par laagu hoti hai.
Order cancel karne par paisa wapas milega?
Ji bilkul. Delivery se pehle tak aap bina kisi charges ke cancel kar sakte hain — full refund. Prepaid orders ka refund 3-5 working days mein original payment method par reflect hota hai (UPI par aam taur par same/next day). COD orders ke liye refund UPI/bank transfer se hota hai.
Payment ke kaun se options hain?
UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, net banking, wallets aur Cash on Delivery — sab supported hain. Saare online payments encrypted (SSL) aur PCI-DSS compliant gateways se hote hain; hum aapke card details kabhi store nahi karte.
Koi item stock mein nahi hai to?
Aise case mein hum aapko pehle inform karte hain — aap substitute accept kar sakte hain, item skip kar sakte hain ya uska refund le sakte hain. Hum kabhi bina bataye koi aur mehanga item nahi bhejte.
Mera area abhi deliver hota hai?
Checkout par apna PIN code/area daalein — turant pata chal jaayega. Agar aapka area list mein nahi hai to WhatsApp (+91 63931 96765) par bata dein — hum aapke area ko jaldi add karne ki koshish karte hain.

Email (support@rkgrocerymart.com): 24 ghante mein andar-andar jawab.

Order mein kuch add/change kar sakta hun?
Picking shuru hone se pehle aap order modify ya cancel kar sakte hain. Picking ke baad change possible nahi hota (taaki aapka slot miss na ho) — aap cancel karke naya order place kar sakte hain, pehle wale ka refund automatically process ho jaata hai.
